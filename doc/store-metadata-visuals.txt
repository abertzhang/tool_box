一、App Store 描述（Description）— 可直接粘贴
====================================================================
【英文版 · 建议直接使用】

Tool Box is an all-in-one everyday toolkit that packs the tools you need most into one lightweight app. No clutter, no dozens of separate apps — just open, use, and go.

Key features:

• Compass — precise bearing, tilt calibration, and your current GPS altitude and coordinates.
• Bubble Level — accurate surface and angle measurement for hanging, leveling, and alignment.
• Sound Meter — measure the ambient noise around you in real time with your microphone.
• Flashlight — a bright flashlight with adjustable brightness.
• Travel Checklist — plan your trip and track every item before you leave.
• Memo — quick notes with editable templates, saved on your device.
• Ruler — measure real-world lengths on your screen.
• Tide — check tide information when you're by the coast.
• Unit Converter — length, area, weight, volume, speed, and temperature.
• Force Converter — quick force unit conversions.
• World Clock — track time across different time zones.

Pro Unlock (one-time purchase):
• Remove all ads.
• Lift the free usage limit and unlock unlimited access to every tool.

Use it free as a guest, or sign in with Apple or Google to keep your Pro benefits across devices. Your data stays on your device — nothing is uploaded.

三、应用图标（App Icon）核对
====================================================================
项目现状：assets/images/ 下有 compass/level/noise/ruler/tide/memo/app.png
         等工具图标；iOS 侧用 Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset 标准尺寸。

App Store 上传要求：
| 项 | 要求 |
|---|---|
| 图标文件 | 1024×1024 像素，无圆角、无透明通道、无边框 |
| 必须使用源图 | 不可仅用截图裁切，需独立设计源图 |
| 大小 | 最大 10 MB，PNG 格式 |
| 内容 | 不能包含 App 名称/文字、水印、iTunes 角标 |

iOS AppIcon.appiconset 内所需尺寸（Xcode 自动生成时对照）：
- iPhone：20/29/40/60 等 @2x/@3x（共约 10 张）
- iPad：20/29/40/76/83.5 等
- 上传时 Xcode 会用 1024 主图自动派生，你只需准备 1 张 1024×1024 PNG。

核对动作：
[ ] 确认 app.png 为 1024×1024 无圆角 PNG
[ ] 图标主体与工具主题一致（建议用 compass 或多工具拼合图形）
[ ] 避免文字、透明、白边
