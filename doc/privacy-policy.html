Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your information when you use this app. This app is a utility toolbox providing a memo, compass, noise detector, level, ruler, flashlight and tide tables. Please read it carefully before you start.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information (optional). The app can be used as a guest. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, we collect your email address along with the nickname and avatar you choose to provide.
  • Usage quota data. To apply the free usage limit, we record tool-open events together with a hashed device identifier (SHA-256, irreversible) on our servers. This is why reinstalling the app does not reset your remaining free uses.
  • Device sensor data. Sensor readings from the compass, accelerometer, gyroscope, microphone and camera are processed on your device only. Audio from the microphone and frames from the camera are never recorded, stored or uploaded.
  • Advertising data. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect an advertising identifier (AAID / IDFA) and related device information to serve and measure ads, as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and maintain your account and sync your profile across devices.
  • To count free tool uses and restore your purchased benefits on another device.
  • To display and measure advertisements in the free version.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve product stability and experience.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

3. Permissions We Request

  • Location (approximate / precise). Used by the compass to correct for magnetic declination and to show true north.
  • Microphone. Used by the noise detector to measure sound level. Audio is analysed in memory and discarded immediately.
  • Camera. Used by the flashlight to switch on the LED / torch. No image is captured or saved.
  • Motion sensors. Used by the level, ruler and compass.

Each permission is requested at runtime only when you open the related tool, and you may revoke any of them at any time in your device settings. Revoking a permission only disables the feature that depends on it.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — serving and measuring advertisements.
  • Supabase — account authentication and cloud database storage.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — processing the Pro Unlock purchase.

Please review their respective privacy policies for how they handle data on our behalf.

5. Data Storage and Security

Account data is stored in a Supabase cloud database and protected with encrypted transfers (HTTPS/TLS) and access control. Local caches such as images stay on your device only, and you can clear them at any time in Settings > Clear Cache.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • Change your nickname and avatar in Edit Profile.
  • Clear local data in Settings > Clear Cache.
  • Delete your cloud profile by deleting your account.
  • Opt out of personalised advertising through your device settings (for example "Opt out of Ads Personalization" in Android settings).
  • In regions where consent is required, change your advertising consent choices at any time via the Ad privacy settings entry in Profile. The app does not request the App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • Request an export or deletion of your data by contacting us below.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date, and we will notify you in the app for significant changes.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please reach us at support@aknot.uk. We will respond within a reasonable period.
